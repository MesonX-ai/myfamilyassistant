Frequently asked questions

Do I need coding experience?

No! The canvas is designed for everyone. No coding required. Just drag and drop.

Can I use my favorite tools?

Yes. We integrate with Google Workspace, Slack, Gmail, and 50+ other services. Can't find yours? Use our webhook API.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Your workflows and data are private by default. You control who sees what. We comply with GDPR and CCPA.

Can I share workflows with my family?

Yes! You can share with view-only access, or collaborate in real-time with edit permissions.

What if something goes wrong?

We have detailed debugging tools to see exactly what happened. Check the logs or re-run with breakpoints.

How much does it cost?

Check our pricing page for plans that fit your needs. We offer free tier for getting started.
