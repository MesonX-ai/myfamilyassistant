AI assistants for every corner of family life

Household Ops

Coordinate chores, bills, and shared calendars across the whole home. The assistant keeps everyone aligned without the group-chat chaos.

Meal Planning

Build weekly menus from dietary needs and what's already in the fridge, then turn them into a shopping list and a one-tap order.

Calendar & Reminders

Natural-language scheduling that resolves conflicts, sends gentle nudges, and never lets a birthday or pickup slip through.

Track allowances, split shared expenses, and surface unusual charges — with guardrails so kids learn healthy money habits.
