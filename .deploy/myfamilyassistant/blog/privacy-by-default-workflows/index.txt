5:T1916,<br /><h1 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; margin: 32px 0 16px; line-height: 1.2;">Privacy by Default: Who Can See Your Workflows</h1><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">We believe privacy is not a feature — it's a responsibility. Here's how we think about it, and what we do to protect your family's data.</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Where Your Data Lives</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">When you build a workflow, data flows through three places:</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">1. **Your browser**: Workflow design, editing, visualization</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">2. **Our servers**: Workflow execution, model calls, persistence</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">3. **Third-party services**: Only if you explicitly connect them (Gmail, calendar, etc.)</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Each layer has different privacy guarantees.</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Your Browser</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">The canvas runs locally. Your workflow definitions, test runs, and the data you preview are stored in your browser cache. We don't transmit them by default.</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">This means:</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">You can build offline</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Your workflow structure stays private until you save</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">We never see a workflow you've only designed and not shared</li><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Our Servers</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">When you save and run workflows, we store:</p><br /><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">The workflow structure (nodes, connections, prompts)</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Execution logs (when your agent ran, how long it took)</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Model outputs (what Claude said, what action was taken)</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">We never store:</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Personally identifiable information, unless you explicitly put it in a prompt</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Message contents from your family's chats</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Passwords or authentication credentials</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We use key rotation and compliance with GDPR/CCPA.</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Sharing & Permissions</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">By default, workflows are private to you. You control who sees them:</p><br /><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Private**: Only you can view and edit</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**View-only**: Share the canvas with family members who can see but not change</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Collaborate**: Invite others to edit your workflow together</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Public**: Share a workflow with the community (you choose what to publish)</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">When you share a workflow, the person you're sharing with can see:</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">The workflow structure and prompts</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Their own execution results</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">NOT other people's data or execution history</li><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Model Calls</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">When your workflow calls Claude or another model:</p><br /><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">We send only the data your prompt requires</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">The model provider (Anthropic, etc.) processes it per their privacy policy</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">We don't log model inputs/outputs by default unless you enable workflow history</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">You can run models on-device if you self-host</li><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Compliance</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">We comply with:</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**GDPR**: You own your data. You can export, modify, or delete any workflow and its history.</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**CCPA**: You have the right to know what we store, who we share it with, and to request deletion.</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**SOC 2 Type II**: Our infrastructure is audited annually. Access is logged.</li><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">What We're Building</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">We're working on:</p><br /><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**End-to-end encryption**: Workflows encrypted so only you can decrypt them</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Local execution**: Run complex workflows entirely on your device</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Differential privacy**: Share aggregate insights without exposing individual data</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Privacy reports**: Transparency about what's flowing where in your workflow</li><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">The Bottom Line</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Your family's automation is yours. We're not mining it for data. We're not selling it.
