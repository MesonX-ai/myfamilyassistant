5:T10ed,<br /><h1 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; margin: 32px 0 16px; line-height: 1.2;">Your First Agent in Five Minutes</h1><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Building an agent has never been easier. In this guide, we'll walk you through creating your first working agent — no coding required.</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">What You'll Build</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">By the end of this guide, you'll have a working agent that:</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Listens for a trigger (a time, event, or manual request)</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Runs a model to make a decision</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Performs an action based on the result</li><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Step 1: Create a New Workflow</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">1. Click **New Workflow** on the canvas</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">2. Give it a name: "My First Agent"</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">3. Choose a template or start blank</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Step 2: Add a Trigger</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Triggers are how your agent knows when to act:</p><br /><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Time-based**: "Every Monday at 9 AM"</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Event-based**: "When a family member texts"</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Manual**: "When I click a button"</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">For your first agent, select **Manual Trigger** — you'll click a button to start it.</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Step 3: Connect a Model</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Models are the brain of your agent. Connect Claude or another LLM:</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">1. Drag a **Model** node onto the canvas</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">2. Connect your trigger to the model</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">3. Write a simple prompt: "Remind me what I need to do today"</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Step 4: Add an Output</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Outputs are where your results go:</p><br /><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Send a notification</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Save to a note</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Post to a group chat</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Log to a spreadsheet</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Connect the model output to an action node.</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Step 5: Test It</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Click **Test Run** to see your agent in action. It should:</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">1. Wait for your trigger (click the button)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">2. Run the model with your prompt</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">3. Perform the output action</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Congratulations! You've built your first agent in under five minutes.</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">What's Next?</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Now that you understand the basics:</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Add more complex logic with conditional nodes</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Connect multiple models for advanced reasoning</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Integrate with your family's tools and services</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Set it to run automatically on a schedule</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Welcome to the studio.
