5:T2773,<br /><h1 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 32px; font-weight: 700; margin: 32px 0 16px; line-height: 1.2;">Meal Planning with Multi-Agent Workflows</h1><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">We were planning weekly meals the old way: texts, voice memos, and spreadsheets scattered across three apps. Then we built this workflow.</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">The Challenge</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Every week, the same dance:</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">1. Mom checks the calendar for busy nights (no time for cooking)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">2. Dad reviews what's in the fridge</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">3. Someone searches for recipes</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">4. Everyone argues about preferences</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">5. Finally, someone writes a shopping list (and forgets something)</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">The Solution: Multi-Agent Workflow</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Instead of humans passing information around, let models work in sequence:</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Agent 1: Calendar Planner**</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Reads your family calendar</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Identifies busy nights (order food, quick meals)</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Flags special occasions (celebrations, guests coming)</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Agent 2: Inventory Manager**</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Checks your grocery inventory</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Notes what's expiring soon (use first!)</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Identifies dietary restrictions</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Agent 3: Meal Generator**</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Takes output from Agents 1 & 2</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Generates meal suggestions for each day</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Considers preferences and time constraints</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Agent 4: Shopping List**</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Takes the meal plan</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Creates a consolidated shopping list</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Groups items by store section</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Removes items you already have</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Agent 5: Communication**</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Shares the plan with your family</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Formats it nicely (dinner theme, special notes)</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Posts to your family group chat</li><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">How It Works</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">The workflow:</p><br /><pre style="background: var(--surface); border: 1px solid var(--border); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; overflow-x: auto; margin: 20px 0;"><code><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Calendar Trigger (Weekly, Sunday 6 PM)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">        ↓</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Read Calendar → (busy nights, events)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">        ↓</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Read Inventory → (available items, expiring soon)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">        ↓</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Generate Meals → (respects constraints, time, preferences)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">        ↓</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Create Shopping List → (consolidated, organized)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">        ↓</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Send Notification → (family sees plan + shopping list)</p><pre style="background: var(--surface); border: 1px solid var(--border); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; overflow-x: auto; margin: 20px 0;"><code><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">The Prompts</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Meal Generator Prompt:**</p><pre style="background: var(--surface); border: 1px solid var(--border); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; overflow-x: auto; margin: 20px 0;"><code><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Plan a week of meals for a family of 4.</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Calendar constraints:</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">{CALENDAR_DATA}</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Available ingredients:</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">{INVENTORY_DATA}</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Preferences:</p><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Vegetarian nights: Monday, Thursday</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Quick meals: Tuesday, Wednesday (under 30 min)</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Celebration dinner: Saturday (guest coming)</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">For each day, provide:</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">1. Meal name</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">2. Time estimate</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">3. Key ingredients needed</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">4. 3-sentence description</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Format as JSON for easy parsing.</p><pre style="background: var(--surface); border: 1px solid var(--border); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; overflow-x: auto; margin: 20px 0;"><code><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Shopping List Prompt:**</p><pre style="background: var(--surface); border: 1px solid var(--border); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; overflow-x: auto; margin: 20px 0;"><code><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Create a shopping list from these meals:</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">{MEAL_PLAN}</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Inventory we have:</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">{CURRENT_INVENTORY}</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Rules:</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">1. Don't include items we already have</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">2. Group by store section (produce, dairy, meat, etc.)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">3. Note quantities</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">4. Flag any specialty items</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">5. Estimate total cost</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Format as a bulleted list.</p><pre style="background: var(--surface); border: 1px solid var(--border); border-radius: 8px; padding: 16px; overflow-x: auto; margin: 20px 0;"><code><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Results</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">After running this workflow for 3 weeks:</p><br /><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**30 minutes saved per week**: No more back-and-forth texts</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Less food waste**: Items expiring soon are prioritized</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Better variety**: The model suggests dishes we wouldn't have thought of</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">**Happier family**: Everyone sees the plan at once and can object early</li><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">How to Build It</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">1. Connect your calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">2. Set up an inventory tracking sheet (manual or RFID scanner)</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">3. Create the meal planning workflow with the prompts above</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">4. Test with this week's data</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">5. Schedule it to run every Sunday</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">Customizations</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Add Nutritional Goals:**</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">"Ensure at least 3 servings of vegetables per day"</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Budget Constraints:**</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">"Keep shopping list under $100"</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Allergy Management:**</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Pull allergies from family profiles and filter recipes</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Restaurant Integration:**</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">On busy nights, suggest local restaurants instead of cooking</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">**Feedback Loop:**</p><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">Every family member rates meals (thumbs up/down) → model learns preferences</p><br /><h2 style="font-family: var(--font-display); font-size: 24px; font-weight: 700; margin: 28px 0 14px; line-height: 1.2;">The Bigger Picture</h2><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">This workflow shows what multi-agent systems can do for families:</p><br /><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">One person (the model) gathers data</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">Another person (model) analyzes constraints</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">A third generates options</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">A fourth formats the output</li><li style="margin-left: 20px; margin-bottom: 8px;">A fifth communicates to humans</li><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">You're not replacing humans with AI — you're using AI to do the coordination work so humans can focus on what matters (deciding if we like the plan, not managing the logistics).</p><br /><p style="margin-bottom: 16px;">That's the real power of agentic workflows.</p><br />8:["slug","meal-planning-multi-agent-workflow","d"]
